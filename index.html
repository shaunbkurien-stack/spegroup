02 · Method

A clear line from evidence to decision

The process is deliberately controlled. The source is checked, the unknowns are exposed, the method is tested, and the conclusion is written so another engineer can follow it.

01

Define the boundary

Confirm the asset, battery limits, interfaces, decision need, deliverables and what is outside the work. Scope ambiguity is resolved before detailed effort begins.

Scope basisBattery limitsInterfaces
02

Verify the evidence

Review drawings, reports, quantities, rates and prior studies. Conflicts are logged. Missing information remains visible instead of being absorbed into an unstated assumption.

Source registerGap reviewAssumptions
03

Test the execution logic

Develop the sequence and check access, lifting, transport, temporary states, worksite constraints and contractor interfaces. Cost and schedule are tested against the method.

ConstructabilityRemoval sequenceCost drivers
04

Issue a controlled basis

State the conclusion, supporting basis, exclusions, uncertainties and actions. The deliverable is written for the client, estimator, contractor and reviewer who must use it next.

05 · Questions

Before a scope is issued

A short asset summary and the decision the work must support are enough for an initial scoping discussion.

What information is needed to start?
The asset type, approximate scale, available information, current project phase and the decision the work must support. Drawings and prior reports can follow after the first discussion.
Can the work be completed under a fixed fee?
Yes, where the scope, source information and deliverable are sufficiently defined. Work with changing inputs or ongoing project interfaces can be completed on an agreed day-rate or capped basis.
How are estimate maturity and uncertainty handled?
The estimate basis records the available definition, quantity basis, rates, productivity, exclusions and risk treatment. An AACE class or accuracy range is only stated where the project basis and relevant guidance support that classification.
Can SPE support an existing owner or contractor team?
Yes. Support can be provided as a defined technical work package or as embedded project engineering through study, tender or delivery phases.
How is project information handled?
Project information is used only for the agreed work and is handled in accordance with the contract and any applicable confidentiality agreement. Named client work is not published without written approval.
Does SPE provide design certification?
Only where expressly included in the agreed scope and completed by a suitably qualified and authorised engineer. General studies, reviews and methodologies should not be read as statutory design certification unless the deliverable states otherwise.
